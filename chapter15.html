Chapter 15: What the Zero Actually Meant

Last chapter we wrote that the Race HUD Kit had 0 downloads. That was wrong, or at least incomplete: the counting itself only started working on Nov 3-4. Before that date, there is no record either way. "0 downloads" was never a measured result; it was a measurement gap mistaken for a number.

Why this matters

We nearly judged a live experiment on a count that was never actually counting. The real verdict, honestly, can only use data from the window when the counter was live: Nov 3-4 onward, through Nov 8.

What we did about it

We asked the owner to check GoatCounter directly on Nov 8: page views on the race-hud page, and the zip-download event count, with a note on when counting began. We also asked RIGHT_HAND to sharpen the landing page itself, leading with the kit's real strength (handling wrong-way, DNF and invalid-lap edge cases) and to put a clearer first-use screenshot above the fold.

What is next

Nov 8: judge the Race HUD Kit on real counted numbers, whatever they are. Then the Gamedev Data Snapshot starts, built from counted Bluesky activity with honest interpretation, not just raw numbers.
