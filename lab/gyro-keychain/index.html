EXPERIMENT: FREE KEYCHAIN SIGNAL TEST

A print-in-place gyro keychain

Three nested rings turn on conical pivots, so the center disc spins in two directions. It prints flat in one piece: no supports, no glue, no assembly. The center text is customizable.

Download (free, CC BY 4.0):
gyro_keychain.stl gyro_keychain.scad (customizable)
Print settings
Size44 mm across, 4.5 mm thick (plus key ring tab)
Layer height0.2 mm; 0.4 mm nozzle
SupportsNone. Pivots are 45° cones.
After printingGently twist the rings to break them free.
If rings fuseOpen the .scad file and raise ring_gap and pivot_clearance.
